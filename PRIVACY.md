# LevelUp Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: August 2026**

## Data Collection
LevelUp stores all data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.

## AI Features
- Chat conversations are processed through OpenRouter API
- No conversation history is stored by our servers
- All chat data remains on your device

## Permissions
- **Internet**: Required for AI chat functionality
- **Storage**: Optional, for PDF viewing only

## Contact
For privacy concerns: Contact through GitHub issues.
